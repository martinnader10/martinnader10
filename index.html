01 / A little about me

Curiosity with

I believe meaningful work begins with asking better questions — then having the patience to build, test and improve the answer.

My interests sit where software, artificial intelligence, product design and problem-solving meet. I enjoy understanding how things work, finding opportunities to make them better, and shaping ideas into experiences people can actually use.

I approach every project as an evolving process: research, plan, create, test, learn and refine. The goal is not simply to make something look impressive — it is to make it useful, thoughtful and memorable.
