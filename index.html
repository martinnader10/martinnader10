ORNVIA

ORNVIA is a clothing marketplace built around a richer relationship between shoppers and sellers. Its planned experience brings together customer and seller accounts, shop profiles, product discovery, orders, sales and payouts, ratings, rewards and personalized AI-assisted recommendations. The long-term direction also explores interactive 3D product viewing and guided product photography.

MedGenius

MedGenius is a smart health-support app concept focused on everyday medication organization. It brings medication information and price lookup, nearby pharmacy discovery, personal medication reminders, confirmation and snooze flows, and a bilingual interface into one clear experience. It is an informational support tool, not a replacement for a doctor or pharmacist.
